# Rollback Rodeo 🚚

A silly, easy-to-play browser game. You're a Sunbelt rollback driver: a big guy with a beard, tattoos, a green cap and a yellow safety vest. Deliver a generator using nothing but the rollback, then head back to the branch. Halfway home, the truck breaks down.

## Play
Open `index.html` in any modern browser. It has no dependencies and needs no build step.

The game is **built for phones held sideways (landscape)**:
- While driving, the whole screen is the road. Hold the red **BRAKE** pedal with your left thumb and the green **GAS** pedal with your right.
- When working the rollback or fixing the truck, the controls sit in a panel on the right. The button for your next step glows.
- On Android, tap ⛶ to go fullscreen. If you hold the phone upright, the game asks you to turn it sideways (or you can tap "Play upright anyway").
- On desktop it works too, with the ← → / A D keys for the pedals and H to honk.

## How it works
0. **Pick a job:** after clocking in, choose between two tickets on the board —
   - **Flatline Lot** *(easy)* — a standard generator, straight back-in drop.
   - **Crooked Creek — Angle Job** *(hard)* — a stretch "long load" generator with DOT flags on each end. The drop zone is roped off at an angle between two poles, so you'll need the **Swing** controls to walk the generator sideways as it comes off the bed and land it inside the zone before it touches down.
1. **Drive to the jobsite:** hold the **GAS** / **BRAKE** pedals. Stop at the stop sign, slow down (35 mph) in the work zone, and park in the green box.
2. **Unload the generator:** use the rollback controls (parking brake, chocks, PTO, chains, bed tilt/slide, winch, cable, and — on the angle job — swing). The clipboard highlights the next step. Tap the winch instead of holding it, or the generator takes the "express route" off the bed. Land it clean with no mistakes and you'll pick up a "Flawless unload" bonus.
3. **Head back to the branch:** watch for Armando the armadillo. When the truck breaks down, put on your hazards and set out triangles, then either **fix it yourself** (inspect, diagnose, and pick the right tool) or **call a tow truck**.

You get points for doing things right and lose points for goofs. Your best score is saved on this device and shown on the title screen — beat it for a "New personal best!" banner (and a little confetti) at the end of a great shift. Your final score also earns you a rank, from *Probationary Rookie* up to *Branch Legend*.
