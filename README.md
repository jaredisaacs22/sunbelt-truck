# Rollback Rodeo 🚚

A silly, easy-to-play browser game. You're a Sunbelt rollback driver: a big guy with a beard, tattoos, a green cap and a yellow safety vest. Deliver a generator using nothing but the rollback, then head back to the branch. Halfway home, the truck breaks down.

## Play
Open `index.html` in any modern browser (desktop or phone). It has no dependencies and needs no build step.

## How it works
1. **Drive to the jobsite:** hold **GAS** / **BRAKE** (or the ← → keys). Stop at the stop sign, slow down in the work zone, and park in the green box.
2. **Unload the generator:** use the rollback controls (parking brake, chocks, PTO, chains, bed tilt/slide, winch, cable). The clipboard highlights the next step. Tap the winch instead of holding it, or the generator takes the "express route" off the bed.
3. **Head back to the branch:** watch for Armando the armadillo. When the truck breaks down, put on your hazards and set out triangles, then either **fix it yourself** (inspect, diagnose, and pick the right tool) or **call a tow truck**.

You get points for doing things right and lose points for goofs. Your final score earns you a rank, from *Probationary Rookie* up to *Branch Legend*.
